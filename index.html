>> how it works

Ten bets a minute. Zero dollars.

  1. 01

    Swipe

    A stack of yes/no questions. Right is YES, left is NO, up skips. Stake 1, 5, 10 or 25 coins. No confirm screens, undo for five seconds.

  2. 02

    See the crowd

    The second you swipe, you see what everyone else said and what you'd win. Then it's trash talk in the thread, with everyone's side on their name.

  3. ¢03

    Called it

    Markets settle like a parimutuel pool: the winners split the pot. Your record, win rate and streak live on your profile, and every win gets a card to share.

Built, tested and open source.

This page is the front door. The full app, with the swipe feed, the play-coin ledger and parimutuel settlement, runs from the code on GitHub. Play coins only: nothing to deposit, nothing to withdraw.
